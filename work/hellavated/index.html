Brand introduction

Built loud
from day one.

Hellavated is a flavor-first cannabis brand with a rebellious streak. Its original identity was raw by design: dark palettes, distressed texture, urban graphics, and a touch of punk.

As the product range grew, the brand needed a broader visual language, one that could carry more formats and flavors without sanding down its edge.

The original signal

Edgy. Urban.
Gritty by intent.

High-contrast color, distressed marks, irreverent illustration, and a little visual noise gave the early system its unmistakable street-level force.

The evolution

The edge stayed.
Everything else
went cosmic.

Saturated galaxies opened the world. Oversized fruit made flavor immediate. Glossy splashes added motion. Brighter color gave every SKU a clear point of recognition.

The central idea

Every flavor
gets its own orbit.

Fruit tells you what to expect. Color makes it easy to find again. Cosmic artwork ties the range together across products and formats.

Packaging system

One universe.
Every format.

Across vape, gummies, concentrates, and infused pre-rolls, the hierarchy stays quick to read: brand first, format second, flavor impossible to miss.

Physical vape design

From package
to palm.

The device was treated as part of the identity, not anonymous hardware. Color, finish, logo placement, and flavor cues carry the packaging story onto the product itself.

Format expansion

Same attitude.
Different form.

The design language stretches beyond vape without losing force. Fruit, cosmic depth, and a consistent information hierarchy keep every product unmistakably part of the same line.

Retail display

Own the counter.

Table tents, posters, and large-format graphics turn individual packs into a full-line statement. The work attracts from a distance, then organizes flavor and format up close.

Environment & exhibition

Built to leave
the shelf.

The refreshed graphic system scales from the smallest pack to wall wraps, display environments, exhibition moments, and social content without losing impact.

Lifestyle

A brand world
with a pulse.

Colored light, cosmic atmosphere, bold styling, and fruit-forward props move the identity from product promise into lived moment.
