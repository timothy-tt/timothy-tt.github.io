The brief / 2024

A familiar blue. A new horizon.

AVITAS had a recognizable color. The next chapter needed a world around it.

I reimagined this Oregon cannabis brand, also present in Washington, for people drawn to trails, mountains, and the coast. Landscape illustration connects the identity, packaging, and website concept through a shared sense of place.

01 / The starting point

Clean, minimal. Unmistakably blue.

The original identity centered on AVITAS Blue, PMS 3265 C—a distinctive turquoise reminiscent of Tiffany Blue. Restrained layouts and white space supported the brand’s messaging around pesticide-free cultivation, natural products, and no additives.

02 / The shift

From a color to a place.

The brief was to make AVITAS more expressive and give it an identity for hikers and nature goers.

I drew on Mountain Dew’s landscape energy and the outdoor character of Patagonia, EMS, and L.L.Bean. Oregon’s mountains, lakes, and coast became the subjects of a more illustrative brand world.

03 / The identity system

Built for the outdoors.

Mountain silhouettes, trees, and a setting sun turn the mark into a trail badge. Wordmarks, framed logos, and compact variations give the identity room to adapt across packaging and digital applications.

The blue stays.
The palette opens up.

AVITAS Blue anchors the system. Ivory, charcoal, and orange bring in the sun, wood, and stone of the outdoor world.

04 / A trail companion

A little character. A lot of personality.

The AVITAS mascot is a simple, stick-figure-like hiker with a backpack and the brand’s blue baseball cap. A recurring companion for the illustrated world, it makes the outdoor idea feel approachable and human.

05 / Packaging

Three landscapes. One AVITAS.

Each product family has its own Oregon setting. Shared color, typography, the orange sun, and a clear label structure hold the range together.

01 / Pre-rolls

Three Sisters.

The mountains wrap around the pre-roll pack, bringing the landscape into every view. The illustration and AVITAS Blue connect the individual package to the wider brand.

02 / Live Resin

Crater Lake.

Mountains and reflected water give Live Resin its own scene. The landscape changes; the shared wordmark, AVITAS Blue, and label structure connect it to the rest of the range.

03 / Ultra

Haystack Rock.

The coast gives Ultra a distinct character. Haystack Rock and the ocean scene bring a new landscape to the system, shown here alongside the gear of a day outdoors.

06 / Beyond the package

A bigger canvas. The same world.

Retail and mural mockups explore the identity at a different scale. Landscape, sun, wordmark, and the Blazing Trails message connect the applications without losing the character of the original illustration.

07 / Website concept

Let curiosity lead the way.

The website idea turns navigation into a hiking trail. An illustrated map leads visitors to products, flower, the brand story, and places to find AVITAS.

Direct navigation stays available for visitors who already know where they want to go. The concept pairs exploration with practical routes to product and location information.

FROM PACKAGING TO INTERFACE

Trail signs become navigation. Wood textures frame content. The same illustrated world carries from the shelf to the screen.
