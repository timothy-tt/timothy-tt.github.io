The Product

The Live Resin all-in-one: landscape wrapped around the device.

Illustrated landscapes carry the identity, one scene per tier.

AVITAS is a full identity system where each product tier owns an illustrated landscape (mountains, ocean, botanicals) in a turquoise-to-navy palette with gold accents, anchored by a distinctive geometric “A” mark for clean, premium shelf presence across Live Resin and Ultra lines.

Deliverables

  • Cartridge & edible label system
  • High-res product renders
  • Logo mark system
  • Brand graphics & illustration
  • Pre-roll & AIO packaging

Visual Identity

Turquoise into deep navy, with gold accents. Each tier owns an illustrated landscape, whether mountains, ocean or botanicals, set in a clean sans-serif with a playful script for line names. The geometric “A” mark holds the whole system together.

The Map

Wayfinding for avitasgrown.com

AVITAS wanted a site you navigate the way you read a trail map. Zegoe drew Oregon as one illustration, made every pin, signpost and board a component with its own hover state, and wrote the motion into the handoff. A separate vendor built it in WordPress.

Motion specification

Hero parallax

  • SkyyPercent 50 → -69.1, then -69.1 → 10Intro 0.8s power1.out, scroll on scrub 0.5
  • MountainyPercent 0 → -60.5, then -60.5 → 0Intro 0.8s power1.out, scroll on scrub 0.5
  • SunyPercent 100 → 0, then 0 → 50Intro 0.5s power1.out, then scroll on scrub true. The one layer that tracks the scroll with no lag
  • ForestyPercent 50 → 0Intro 0.8s power1.out. No scroll tween, so the foreground holds still
  • Triggerstart top top-=20%, end bottom centerGSAP 3.12.5 with ScrollTrigger. The parallax is built only above 981px

Map ambience and states

  • sunshine6s ease-in-out infiniteOpacity 1 at 30%, 0 at 50%, 1 at 70%, alternate paths delayed 3s. 15 cloud and sun-ray paths
  • wind, wind-26s ease-in-out infinitetranslateX 15px and -15px at the halfway keyframe, alternating across 16 paths
  • move-circle15s linear, and 10s linear reverseNine keyframes tracing a 10px circle. Blue balloon at 15s, red balloon at 10s reversed
  • Pin hoverfill #00C4B3 and #89C9BEtransition all 0.3s ease-in-out on the paths inside each pin
  • button-tab3s ease-in-out infinitetranslateY -2px at 5% and again at 15%, then flat from 20% to the end
  • Signpost labelopacity 0 → 1 on hoverAbove 981px the landmark name stays hidden until the signpost is hovered
  • Breakpointmax-width 767pxThe interactive map is hidden and the wooden board carries the wayfinding alone

Zegoe’s handoff asked for the motion. These numbers were read back off the built theme’s stylesheet and its GSAP setup, so this is the specification as it shipped.
