Small device.
Big lore.

That split second before the loot chest opens? That was our starting point.

Lost Lake’s brief brought together accessible pricing in Oregon and adult gaming culture. We built the visual world around that combo: a compact wordmark, RGB color and a different cast of lettering for every flavor.

02 / BRAND STRATEGYTHE BUILD BEHIND THE GLOW

Behind
the glow.

Every good game world has its own logic. Ours starts with a recognizable core, then gives the artwork room to go a little extra.

01

Positioning

The brief: accessible price. Big visual presence.

Lost Lake placed Loot Bar in Oregon’s competitive middle and lower price range. We answered with a compact identity and expressive artwork built around gaming references.

02

Central idea

The chest-opening moment.

The glowing chest is our recurring scene. Light spills out, details stack up, and each flavor gets its own reveal. That little moment of “wait, what’s in there?” drives the artwork.

03

Recognition

Same character. Different skins.

The wordmark and chest stay in the party. Color, lettering and imagery switch skins from one flavor to the next, keeping the family recognizable while every pack gets its own personality.

04

Cultural reference

Straight from the setup.

The original brief centered on adult men, 21+, with gaming interests. We pulled from that visual vocabulary: RGB peripherals, pixel interfaces and the oversized lettering of game title screens.

THE BRAND STORY

LORE,
UNLOCKED.

We started with the chest-opening moment and built the scene around it. The chest gives the artwork a story; the light makes the reveal. From there, every flavor gets a different setting, color palette and title treatment. Same universe. Plenty of side quests.

03 / IDENTITYFORM STUDY

Hardware.
Meet wordmark.

The plot twist: the “b” was hiding in the hardware. We stripped the device back to its silhouette, turned its raised shoulder into the letter’s curve, and used the window as the opening. Then the “b” joined the rest of the wordmark.

FORM EVOLUTION01 / BLUEPRINT

Blueprint → silhouette → “b” → wordmark. Original vectors, with a new animation connecting the dots.

04 / VISUAL DIRECTIONMOODBOARD / EXTENDED DIRECTION

Pixels after dark.

We pulled from the setup: RGB peripherals, prismatic surfaces and a portrait with a little signal interference. Deep shadows set the stage. Color gets the main-character lighting.

Dark mode, always

Black is the base layer. Neon edges and saturated color get the spotlight.

Pixels with an afterglow

Crisp squares meet soft light spill. A little screen nostalgia goes a long way.

Texture settings: high

Crystal facets, shifting reflections and matte fabric keep the world from feeling flat.

08 / SWAGCONCEPT EXTENSION

The IRL
loadout.

We took the identity off-screen and onto hats, tees and hoodies. A small embroidered “b”, a bold chest mark and a fractured back graphic give each piece its own role in the same apparel lineup.

09 / REFLECTIONLOOT BAR / LOST LAKE

GG.
WORLD BUILT.

The chest was the starting point. The rest became a whole design universe.

We gave each flavor its own title-screen energy, with a shared wordmark and chest keeping the party together. Different palettes, textures and lettering. Still unmistakably part of the same build.

And because world-building tends to come with side quests, we explored a space and a wearable collection too. Those are new concept studies; the original identity and packaging made it into the real world.
