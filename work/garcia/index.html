TRACK 01 / THE BRIEF

AN ICONIC VOICE, CARRIED FORWARD.

Garcia Hand Picked arrived with a rich vocabulary already in motion: Jerry's handprint, portraiture, marbled color, music references, and a generous sense of individuality. My task was to carry that voice into new markets and physical formats without mistaking adaptation for ownership.

The creative challenge was one of discernment. Every solution needed to feel discovered within the brand rather than placed on top of it, while still resolving contemporary packaging, campaign, compliance, and retail demands.

TRACK 02 / CREATIVE STEWARDSHIP

THIS WAS NOT A REBRAND.

The identity was the master recording.

Working inside an inherited system is a different kind of creativity. I identified which cues carried the brand's authorship, then built around them through proportion, hierarchy, color, material, and production decisions.

The discipline was knowing when to introduce a new solution and when the most intelligent choice was to let the original identity lead.

SIDE A / CALIFORNIA PRESSING

BUILT FOR CALIFORNIA. TRUE TO GARCIA.

California brought its own regulatory, production, and retail requirements, but the package still needed to remain unmistakably Garcia. Working with Holistic Industries, I adapted dielines, information hierarchy, compliance content, and SKU distinctions while protecting the brand's familiar visual voice.

TRACK 04 / THE PRODUCT FAMILY

PACKAGING, PLAYED ACROSS THE FULL SET.

Flower jars, pre-roll tubes, cartons, and supporting formats each required their own hierarchy and production decisions. The system had to stay clear at arm's length, cohesive in a group, and recognizable when surrounded by the visual noise of a working dispensary.

SIDE B / LOS ANGELES

LOS ANGELES, PLAYED IN A NEW KEY.

The LA campaign formed a separate creative chapter within the California work. I contributed campaign key art and packaging that interpreted the established Garcia language through a city-specific lens, creating a local expression without severing the brand from its roots.

TRACK 05 / IN THE WILD

PACKAGING MADE TO BE SEEN IN REAL LIFE.

Lifestyle and event photography gives the work scale and materiality. It shows the package as part of a social, tactile experience, surrounded by education, ritual, conversation, and the charming disorder of a live event.

TRACK 06 / CONCEPT TO COUNTER

CONCEPTS YOU COULD WALK INTO.

Three-dimensional visualization made it possible to test scale, product density, sightlines, lighting, and brand hierarchy before production. The concepts range from portable activation pieces to dedicated shelf units and a broader multi-brand retail environment.

A brand can be faithful and still travel.

The strongest concepts do not merely enlarge a package. They identify which parts of the identity can become architecture, which details guide navigation, and where restraint allows the product to remain the focus.

ENCORE / THE RESULT

THE MAGIC WAS IN THE TRANSLATION.

The brand was already beloved. My task was to make each new expression feel as though it had always belonged. Across packaging, campaign work, photography, and dimensional retail concepts, the project demonstrates what thoughtful stewardship can do: preserve the soul, solve the practical problem, and give an established voice new places to be heard.
